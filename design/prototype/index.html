My working agreements

Every team I join gets a working agreement. This is mine.

Start with the question.

Three and a half years at Kantar, coordinating market research, taught me that every project exists to answer something. Before any sprint, I want to know what we're solving, and for whom.

Make the work visible.

Boards, dashboards and plain status updates, so nobody has to ask where things stand. Leaders get the truth early, good or bad.

Clear the path.

My job is to spot risks and blockers before they slow the team down, and to keep releases calm with clear go/no-go checks.
